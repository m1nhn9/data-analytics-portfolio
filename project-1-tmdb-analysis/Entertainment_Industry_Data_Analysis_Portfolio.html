I · Content Strategy Platform Acquisition

Popularity is a weak predictor of content quality

0.079
Pearson r (n = 3,981)
Spearman ρ = 0.230

Across 3,981 records with ≥ 50 votes, the correlation between TMDB popularity score and audience rating is r = 0.079. Popularity explains under 1% of rating variance. Because popularity is heavily skewed (a few titles score in the hundreds), the rank-based Spearman correlation is also reported: ρ = 0.230, a weak positive relationship. On either measure, trending position is a poor guide to audience satisfaction.

IV · Audience Insight Read With Caution

Recent releases rate lower, but vote counts and survivorship explain much of the gap

3,116 → 115
median votes · 2018 vs 2026 releases

Average ratings peaked at 7.16 for 2020 releases and are 6.66 for 2026 releases. This does not show that audiences are rating more harshly. Recent films have far fewer votes (median 3,116 for 2018 releases vs 115 for 2026), and early ratings often shift as more people vote. Older films also only appear in a current popularity list if people are still watching them, which favours well-regarded titles (survivorship). The higher average popularity of recent releases reflects recency, not a rise in demand.

Implication
A discovery shelf surfacing titles with ratings above 8.0 and below-median popularity is a low-cost retention mechanism.
