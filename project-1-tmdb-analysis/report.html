Q1
Does popularity actually mean quality?
Content Acquisition Platform Strategy
r = 0.079
ρ = 0.230

Weak correlation

Pearson r and Spearman rank ρ on 3,981 reliable films (votes ≥ 50). Popularity is heavily skewed, so the rank-based Spearman is the fairer measure: it shows a weak positive link. Either way, viral content does not reliably satisfy audiences.

Business Recommendation
Streaming platforms should decouple their homepage trending algorithm from their content acquisition strategy — the data shows viral content does not reliably deliver audience satisfaction.

Q4
Are audiences rating movies more harshly in recent years?
Audience Insight Quality Benchmarking
Average ratings peaked 2020–2022 (~7.0+) and are lower for recent releases, but this is not evidence that audiences rate more harshly. Recent films have far fewer votes (median 3,116 for 2018 releases vs 115 for 2026), and older films only stay in a current popularity list if people still watch them (survivorship).
